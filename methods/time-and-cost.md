# Turn time saved into a defensible benefit estimate

Define the smallest unit where the workflow can change and the population that is eligible. If the question concerns intended work that never happened, start with a schedule or assignment record and join observed activity. Execution logs alone can miss those cases.

Measure time, throughput, and service quality separately. When examining a ratio, inspect numerator and denominator individually. Less recorded time can raise output per hour even when output stays flat.

In an invented example, 1,000 eligible tasks save an average of 30 seconds each, equivalent to about 8.3 hours of capacity. To estimate cash savings, show how that capacity would change actual paid hours, overtime, or expenses. State who can realize the change and over what period. Check the estimate after rollout against observed adoption and operating constraints.
