# Trace a claim to its evidence

1. Write the exact claim and the decision it could affect.
2. Open the source. Record its date, version, author or owner, and the location that supports the claim. A snippet can help find the source.
3. Identify what the source establishes. A working proposal can establish intent. A test result can establish behavior under the tested conditions. A deployment record can establish when a change shipped.
4. For a quantitative claim, keep the population, denominator, window, unit, and missing data beside the result.
5. If sources disagree, describe the specific conflict and check which definition, period, or decision each source governs.

Example: a plan targets a two-day turnaround. A sample of completed work gives a median of three days. Record the target and observation separately, with collection dates and the sample definition.
